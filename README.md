### Methodology

This model employs the **Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF)** approach with:

- **Valuation Framework**: Two-stage DCF model
- **Terminal Value Method**: Perpetuity Growth Model
- **Discount Rate**: Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)
- **Projection Period**: 6 years (2025-2030)
- **Terminal Growth Rate**: 7%

### Cost Components

### WACC Formula


## 🎓 DuPont Analysis

### ROE Decomposition (2024)

Amazon's exceptional 26.78% ROE is driven by:

```
ROE = Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
26.78% = 12.01% × 1.02 × 2.19
```

**Key Insights:**
- **Net Margin (12.01%)**: Profitability driver - doubled from 2020 (5.53%)
- **Asset Turnover (1.02)**: Slight decline due to infrastructure investments
- **Equity Multiplier (2.19)**: Conservative leverage, down from 3.44 (2020)

### ROA Decomposition (2024)

```
ROA = Net Margin × Asset Turnover
12.26% = 12.01% × 1.02
```

The 12.26% ROA represents Amazon's efficiency in generating profits from total assets, showing strong improvement from the 2022 crisis period (-0.6%).
